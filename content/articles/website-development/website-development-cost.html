Website development cost in India ranges from about ₹15,000 for a basic brochure site to ₹15 lakh or more for a complex ecommerce store or custom web app. The price depends on scope, features, integrations, design complexity, platform and maintenance. Ask for itemised quotes, and budget 15 to 20 percent of the build cost each year for upkeep.

What affects the cost of a website?

  • Number of pages and content. More pages mean more design, writing and setup.
  • Design. A customised theme costs less than a fully bespoke design.
  • Features. Booking, logins, payments, search and dashboards add work.
  • Integrations. CRM, ERP, WhatsApp, payment gateways and APIs.
  • Platform. WordPress, Shopify or a custom build.
  • Languages and SEO. Multilingual content and SEO setup add effort.
  • Maintenance and hosting. Ongoing costs after launch.

Ongoing costs: maintenance, hosting and redesign

Website maintenance in India usually costs ₹1,500 to ₹25,000 a month, depending on scope. First, basic plans cover updates, backups and security. Then advanced plans add speed tuning, content updates and SEO monitoring. For example, a small WordPress site may need only ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 a year for hosting and maintenance. How long does website development take?

A simple site takes two to four weeks. Next, a business or ecommerce site takes six to twelve weeks. Finally, complex web apps take three to six months. Delays usually come from late content, slow approvals and changing requirements, so plan content and decisions early.

How to reduce website cost without cutting quality

  1. Write a clear brief with must-have and nice-to-have features.
  2. Start with the pages that bring leads, and add more later.
  3. Use a proven theme or platform where custom work adds little value.
  4. Have your content and photos ready before development starts.
  5. Choose fixed-price or milestone-based contracts.

Common website pricing mistakes

Watch out for quotes that skip maintenance, hosting or SEO setup. Avoid agencies that won't itemise the work or give you ownership of your code and domain. Always compare what is included, not just the headline price.

How to keep website development cost under control

First, write a clear brief before you ask for quotes, because vague scope is the main reason costs rise. Next, ask every company for an itemised quote, so you can compare design, development, content, SEO and support line by line. Then split the project into phases. For example, launch the core pages first and add advanced features later. In addition, check what you will pay after launch, such as hosting, CMS licences, plugin renewals and maintenance. Also keep a small reserve for changes, since new ideas always appear during a build. As a result, the website development cost stays predictable, and you pay for features that bring real value.

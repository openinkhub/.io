What is an SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO audit?

Search has changed. People still use Google, but many now ask AI tools for answers instead. So a modern audit has to cover four layers, not one:

  • SEO (search engine optimization): can Google crawl, understand and rank your pages?
  • AEO (answer engine optimization): do your pages give short, clear answers that win featured snippets, "People also ask" boxes and voice results?
  • GEO (generative engine optimization): do AI assistants mention, cite and recommend your business when people ask about your services?
  • AIO (artificial intelligence optimization): is your brand data structured and consistent, so machines can read it and AI tools describe you correctly?

In short, the audit shows whether you are visible wherever your customers search, from a Google results page to a chatbot answer.

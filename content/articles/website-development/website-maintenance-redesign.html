Website maintenance keeps your site secure, fast and up to date after launch. A maintenance checklist covers software and plugin updates, security patches, backups, uptime and malware monitoring, speed checks, broken-link fixes, content refreshes, SEO reviews and form testing. Apply security updates at least monthly, and review content and SEO every quarter.

Website maintenance checklist: what to update and how often

Why website maintenance matters

  • Security. Most hacks use known holes that an update would have closed.
  • Speed. Sites slow down over time as content and plugins pile up.
  • Rankings. Fresh, working pages keep search engines and visitors happy.
  • Leads. A broken form can quietly cost you enquiries for weeks.

Website backup and recovery best practices

  1. Back up automatically, as often as your content changes.
  2. Keep copies off the server, in a separate location.
  3. Keep several versions, not just the latest one.
  4. Test a restore regularly, and write down the steps.

Website hosting vs website maintenance

Hosting is the server, storage and network your site runs on. Maintenance is the ongoing care: updates, security, backups, speed tuning and support. You need both, and they often come from different providers. Be clear about who is responsible for what.

What can you do yourself?

Many businesses update their own text, images and blog posts through the CMS. Leave software updates, security, backups and speed work to someone who can test changes safely and fix problems quickly.

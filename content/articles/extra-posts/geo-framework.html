GEO Framework: A 6-Step Plan to Get Cited by AI Answers

A GEO framework is a repeatable plan for generative engine optimization: making your brand easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, trust and cite. Our GEO framework has six steps: audit, entity, answers, evidence, structure and tracking. Most businesses can run the first full cycle in about 30 days.

This guide explains each step, what to check, and how GEO fits with SEO, AEO and AIO.

Why you need a GEO framework

AI tools now answer many questions before anyone clicks a link. So if your brand is not part of those answers, buyers may never see you. A framework helps in three ways:

  • It turns GEO from guesswork into a checklist your team can repeat
  • GEO work then links to the SEO, AEO and AIO work you already do
  • A monthly score shows your progress

Step-by-step: running the GEO framework

Step 1: Audit your AI visibility

First, list the questions buyers ask before they choose a firm like yours. Then ask each one in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Note whether you are named, which rivals appear and which sources are cited.

Step 2: Fix your entity data

AI tools merge facts from many sites. So your brand name, address, phone and service list must match on your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories.

Step 3: Write answer-first content

Use real questions as H2 or H3 headings. Then answer each one in the first 40–60 words, and add detail below.

Step 4: Add evidence and mentions

Publish your own numbers, client results and expert quotes. Also, earn mentions on trusted industry sites, because AI tools lean on sources others already trust.

Step 5: Structure pages for machines

Add Organization, Service and FAQ schema, and test it in Google's Rich Results Test. Keep pages fast, and consider an llms.txt file that lists your key pages. Google's own guide to AI features and your website confirms that normal SEO best practice still applies.

Step 6: Track and repeat

Finally, rerun your question list every month. Track how often you are named and cited, then fix the gaps you find.

These approaches support each other. For example, strong SEO pages are more likely to be used as AI sources, and clean AIO data helps GEO work faster.

How to choose where to start

  • AI tools describe you wrongly: start with Step 2, entity data.
  • Rivals are named and you are not: start with Steps 3 and 4, answers and evidence.
  • You have content but no schema: start with Step 5, structure.
  • You are not sure: start with Step 1 and let the audit decide.

Frequently asked questions

What is a GEO framework?

It is a step-by-step plan for generative engine optimization. It helps AI tools find, trust and cite your brand in their answers.

How long does the GEO framework take?

The first full cycle usually takes about 30 days. After that, a monthly check keeps it up to date.

Do I need SEO before GEO?

Yes, ideally. AI tools often draw on pages that already rank and load well, so SEO gives GEO a strong base.

How do I measure GEO results?

Ask the same buyer questions in AI tools each month. Then track how often your brand is named, which pages are cited and how accurate the answers are.

To start an ecommerce website in India, choose a platform such as Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom build. Next, register your business and GST, and set up payments through a gateway such as Razorpay, PayU or Stripe. Then arrange shipping, build your catalogue and product pages, add policy pages, and launch with SEO, analytics and marketing in place. A typical budget runs from about ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh or more, depending on scope.

This guide walks you through each step to start an ecommerce website in India.

How do you start an ecommerce website in India? (8 steps)

  1. Pick your niche and products. Check demand, margins and competitors.
  2. Register your business. Choose a structure, open a current account and register for GST where required.
  3. Choose a platform. Compare hosted, open-source and custom options (see below).
  4. Set up payments. Integrate a payment gateway that supports UPI, cards, net banking and wallets.
  5. Plan shipping. Use a shipping aggregator or courier partners, and decide on cash on delivery.
  6. Build your catalogue. Write clear product titles, descriptions, specs and photos.
  7. Add trust and policy pages. Include returns, refunds, shipping, privacy, terms and contact details.
  8. Launch and market. Set up SEO, analytics, Google Merchant Center and your first campaigns.

How do you handle payments in India?

Indian shoppers use many payment methods. So your gateway should support UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and popular wallets. In addition, check settlement times, fees, refund handling and international card support if you sell abroad.

Many stores also offer cash on delivery. However, COD increases returns and failed deliveries, so some stores add a small COD fee or verify orders first.

How do you set up shipping?

Shipping aggregators connect you with several couriers through one dashboard. As a result, you can compare rates, print labels and track orders in one place. Also decide on free-shipping thresholds, delivery zones and packaging early, because they affect your margins.

What does GST mean for ecommerce?

Online sellers in India generally need to understand GST registration, invoicing and tax collected at source by marketplaces. Rules can change, so confirm your position with a qualified tax adviser. The official GST portal is the primary source for registration and returns.

How much does an ecommerce website cost in India?

Costs depend on the platform, design, number of products, integrations and marketing. Roughly, a simple store on a hosted platform sits at the lower end, while custom builds with ERP or marketplace links sit much higher. How do you get your first customers?

  • Optimise product and category pages for search.
  • List products in Google Merchant Center.
  • Use Instagram and WhatsApp to reach early buyers.
  • Collect reviews from your first orders.

What are common ecommerce launch mistakes?

  • Launching with thin product descriptions and poor photos.
  • Skipping clear return and refund policies.
  • Choosing a platform you will outgrow within a year.
  • Ignoring mobile speed, even though most shoppers buy on phones.

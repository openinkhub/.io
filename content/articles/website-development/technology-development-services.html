How to choose the right technology stack

  1. Start from the job the site must do: inform, sell, or run a service.
  2. Check SEO and speed needs: public pages need server or static rendering.
  3. Consider your team: who will maintain it after launch?
  4. Plan for growth, security and budget, both upfront and ongoing.

Common, proven stacks include Next.js with Node.js, the MERN stack, and Laravel or Django with a React or Vue front end. See full stack and databases for details.
